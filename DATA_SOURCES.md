# Data Sources

## Selection rules

Prefer traceable primary documents and accountable institutional publications. Secondary reporting may provide context but must be labelled. Keep summaries original, brief, neutral, and narrower than the linked source supports. Never infer casualty totals, identity, intent, or quotations.

## Starter registry

1. [OHCHR fact-finding report](https://www.ohchr.org/en/documents/country-reports/ohchr-fact-finding-report-human-rights-violations-and-abuses-related) — institutional report.
2. [OHCHR preliminary analysis](https://www.ohchr.org/en/documents/country-reports/preliminary-analysis-recent-protests-and-unrest-bangladesh) — preliminary institutional analysis.
3. [Access Now statement](https://www.accessnow.org/press-release/keepiton-restore-internet-during-student-protests-bangladesh/) — civil-society public statement concerning internet access.

## Adding or replacing a source

Open the original URL, confirm publisher/date/context, add bilingual original summaries and source type in `src/data.ts`, link corroborating records, label uncertainties, and test every ID. Only `http`/`https` URLs are valid. An archived link should come from a reputable preservation service and never replace the original URL.

## Consent, copyright, and demo data

Obtain informed consent for personal testimony and sensitive media; record reuse limits. Minimise identifying details and never treat public availability as unlimited permission. Link to copyrighted media rather than copying it unless licence/permission is documented. Keep `demoOnly: true` until human editorial review is complete.
